GDG AITR Portal Review & Suggestions

I reviewed the GDG AITR portal on a mobile device to evaluate its layout, navigation, login interface, and overall user experience.

2. Positive Observations

- The website has a clean and consistent dark theme.
- The navigation menu clearly displays the main sections.
- The homepage buttons and statistics cards are properly visible.
- The login interface has clearly labeled fields and buttons.
- The footer provides contact information and quick navigation links.

3. Suggestions for Improvement

- Further optimize vertical spacing on mobile screens where appropriate.
- Continue testing responsiveness across different screen sizes and devices.
- Maintain accessible color contrast, readable text, and descriptive labels.

4. Conclusion

The portal provides a well-organized and user-friendly mobile experience. The suggestions above could further improve accessibility, responsiveness, and usability.
